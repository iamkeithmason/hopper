Save button on images

Applies to pages opened after you change it.

Ways to save

  • Toolbar button or Alt+Shift+H: the page, with a note, any selected text, and an optional screenshot.
  • Right-click: save the page, an image, a link, selected text as a quote, or a screenshot of the view.
  • Hover over an image: the Save button.
